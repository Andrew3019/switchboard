# ask-dont-guess

Stop and ask rather than proceeding on an assumption:

- A tool or command fails twice in a row.
- The task is ambiguous and two readings would produce different work.
- You are about to do work you were told to delegate.
- You are about to do something hard to undo: push, merge, delete, or rewrite history.

Use `sb block "<what you need>"`. Guessing produces work that looks finished and is
wrong, which costs more than the question. (`sb ask` is for other agents; the human has
no mailbox, so `sb ask human` is refused.)
