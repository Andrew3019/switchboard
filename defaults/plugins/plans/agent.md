# plans

- Is your work heading for a change that will land? `sb plugin plans guide` says which of a plan, a change record, or neither this one wants — a plan only where the work has to be shaped and approved first, a record where a bounded change needs somewhere to keep its verification, review, PR and landing approval. Investigation, review, questions, anything you just answer and report, and everything a dispatcher does need neither.
